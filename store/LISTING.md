# Play Store listing — MORSE

## Short description (max 80 chars)

Learn and translate Morse code: quizzes, words and a handy translator.

## Full description

MORSE helps you learn the Morse alphabet and translate messages in both
directions.

TRANSLATE
• Text → Morse with live translation — long-press to copy the result
• Morse → Text with the dedicated . – / keypad

LEARN
• CHOOSE — pick the right translation among 4 answers and beat your
  high score
• ∞ CHOOSE — the endless mode with a cumulative score
• WORDS — decode whole words in morse
• TYPE — write the translation yourself
• Practice with letters, morse prompts, or both

All scores stay on your device: no accounts, no ads, no tracking.
